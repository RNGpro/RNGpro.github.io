What does this mean?

R-scores is the way to measure your account's luck over all its aspects like average roll EP, median roll EP, best roll EP, worst roll EP, and longest streak with/without all the rarities. The lower it is, the unluckier you are, and the higher it is, the luckier you are. 0 r-scores means EVERYTHING is on par with the expected values, and it is meant to emulate z-scores, so values can be negative. That said, here are some rough thresholds:

  • less than -5 r-scores: astronomically unlucky
  • -5.0000 to -4.0000 r-scores: extremely unlucky
  • -4.0000 to -3.0000 r-scores: brutally unlucky
  • -3.0000 to -2.0000 r-scores: very unlucky
  • -2.0000 to -1.0000 r-scores: unlucky
  • -1.0000 to 0.0000 r-scores: worse than expected
  • 0.0000 r-scores: on par
  • 0.0000 to 1.0000 r-scores: better than expected
  • 1.0000 to 2.0000 r-scores: lucky
  • 2.0000 to 3.0000 r-scores: very lucky
  • 3.0000 to 4.0000 r-scores: insanely lucky
  • 4.0000 to 5.0000 r-scores: extremely lucky
  • more than 5.0000 r-scores: astronomically lucky
